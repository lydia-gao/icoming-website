ABOUT US
 
 
 
 
Pingyang Icom Bag Co., Ltd.
Pingyang iCom Bag Co., Ltd., founded in 2006, is an industrial and trade company specializing in the research, development, production and sales of shopping bags, non-woven bags, packaging bags, suit covers and take away insulation bags.

The head quarters of the company is located in Pingyang, Wenzhou, with the production department, quality inspection department, operation department, business department, and other departments.
 
The company used to be a pure foreign trade company, then took the advice of the salesman, based on the foreign factory inspection, proofing, cost requirements, set up a factory. 
 
 
The factory is located in Pingyang County, and the business department is located in Wenzhou City. At present, the factory has 2000 square meters, 30 workers, 2 teams of 11 people in the business department.
