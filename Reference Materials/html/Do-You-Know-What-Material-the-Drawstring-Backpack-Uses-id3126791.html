Do You Know What Material the Drawstring Backpack Uses?

Views: 200     Author: Site Editor     Publish Time: 2021-03-16      Origin: Site

Drawstring bags can be made of many materials. Different materials have different advantages and disadvantages, depending on how long and how long you want to use them. Most of the materials used in drawstring bags are flannel, nylon, non-woven fabrics, etc. The drawstring bags with flat drawstring cords are flexible and convenient to use. The product is light and portable, which feels soft and gentle.

 

 


Linen

Burlap is a durable, easy-to-maintain material. Many canvas bags are made of this material, which is easy to customize and environmentally friendly. It has also been used to make other handicrafts for centuries. 

 

The advantage of linen is that it is environmentally friendly, biodegradable, and very durable. But its shortcomings are also obvious. Linen is a darker material, so if you want to have colorful drawstring bags, linen drawstring bags are not ideal. At the same time, linen drawstring bags are not resistant to water.

 

 

Cotton

Cotton is another popular material for making drawstring bags. It is easy to maintain, and the elastic material can help the bag withstand daily wear and tear. Cotton drawstring bags are also environmentally friendly, and old shirts can be recycled.

 

The cotton drawstring polyester pouches are elastic and can store more things. However, cotton is usually a thinner material and may be difficult to sew.

 

 

Canvas

Canvas materials are becoming more and more popular in drawstring bag construction. This is an inexpensive option, and the paint can be easily customized. Canvas is also known for maintaining its flexibility.

 

Canvas is not only cheap but also easy to customize with paint or markers. Therefore, if you want to design your unique drawstring bag, then you can choose a canvas drawstring bag. Of course, the canvas has its shortcomings. As a harder material, canvas may be difficult to sew by hand.

 

 

Mesh

For more bags used indoors, the mesh is a very cheap and breathable material. It comes in many colors and is very lightweight. The mesh is very light and woven tighter. However, there is the possibility of tearing the hole and it won't let your things dry out.

 

 


Nylon

Another very popular material for drawstring backpacks is nylon. This smooth material has a variety of colors and is more resistant to pollution. Besides, nylon is also easy to maintain. At the same time, it is tightly woven and can be water-resistant. However, nylon drawstring backpack can melt under the high heat of the dryer and is not biodegradable.

 

 

Polyester

Polyester is a durable fabric that is resistant to fading. It is a lightweight fabric that is resistant to wrinkles and stains. You can wash and dry this fabric under low heat. The polyester drawstring bag can keep the color for a long time. However, this drawstring polyester bag cannot be impermeable to air and tends to retain odor.

 

 

Various logos can be printed on the surface of the customized recycle polyester folding drawstring bag according to the needs of customers, and the size of the promotional drawstring bag can be set according to the specifications of the packaged product. Therefore, the application and versatility of drawstring bags are greatly enriched. Due to their environmentally friendly and durable materials, drawstring bags have become a kind of environmentally friendly bag nowadays and are widely used in gifts, jewelry, mobile phones, shopping, and other applications.
