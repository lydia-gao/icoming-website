What You Should Know about When Customize Cooler Bags？

Views: 268     Author: Anna     Publish Time: 2021-02-20      Origin: Site

We often say that the ice packs for coolers can be used to hold all kinds of food and keep food fresh, because it can be used for outdoor picnics or daily life, which belongs to a kind of outdoor bags.

 

 


What Are the Uses of Thermal Insulation Bags?

 

Insulation bag is widely used in daily life, which has good cold, heat, and preservation function. The coolers & insulated bag can be used in large quantities for the well-known take out delivery, which can also be used to carry working meals, iced fruits in summer, milk tea and coffee in winter, seafood distribution, camping dinner, etc. Besides, it can occasionally be used for refrigerated transportation of drugs and vaccines, etc.

 

With the improvement of living standards, consumers' pursuit of quality, and the promotion of brands by enterprises, soft cooler bags are made to order in batches, which can realize the printing of brand advertisements on the bags and make their customers feel the special culture of the enterprise. For products that need comfortable temperature to keep freshness, insulated shopping bags can make enterprises achieve better brand promotion and enhance their brand influence.

 

 

What Are the Materials of Insulation Bag?

 

On the market (especially for enterprises as promotional gifts), the most common materials of picnic cooler bags are non-woven fabric + pearl cotton aluminum film, followed by Oxford cloth, polyester, DuPont paper, Kraft paper, and other materials.

 

 

What Are the Styles of Insulation Bags?

l Zipper cross insulation bags

l Portable messenger insulation bags

l DuPont paper handle chain insulation bags

 

 


What Are the Colors of the Insulation Bag?

 

There are many colors in the best cooler bag, so the daily color matching can basically meet the needs of 80% of customers. Also, you can choose according to the quantity and budget as follows:

 

1. If you belong to a small number of customers, you can choose the color of common fabrics in the market;

2. If you need mass customization of insulated tote bags, you can customize spot color fabrics according to customers' demand for color;

3. Customers can provide color number to businesses, so as to obtain the closest physical effect;

4. According to customer requirements, businesses can provide several close physical or color cards for customers to select.

 

 

Size of Insulation Bag

 

Due to the particularity of customization, the size of fish cooler bags is usually customized according to the needs of customers. If you want to quickly select the right size, you can also follow these two methods:

 

1. According to the requirements of customers, we provide several different sizes of sample bags for selection and try;

2. According to the content, we tailor the style and size of the beach cooler bag;

 

To sum up, the printing process of travel cooler bag needs to be determined according to the actual needs of customers, specific content, and design documents.
