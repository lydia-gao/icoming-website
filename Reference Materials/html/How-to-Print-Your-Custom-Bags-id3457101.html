How to Print Your Custom Bags?

Views: 246     Author: Site Editor     Publish Time: 2021-08-20      Origin: Site

Nowadays, more and more people choose reusable shopping bags in daily life, which also promote a lot of companies to customize different shopping bags for advertisement. Then how to print your custom shopping bags after design the pattern? In order to help you to choose the right printing technique, here we will talk about printing methods of different custom bags.

 

Usually, there are two kinds of printing inks for you to choose, one is water-based elastic glue and the other is ink. For example, the printing on ordinary non-woven bags is made of water-based elastic glue. The biggest feature of this kind of water-based elastic glue is that the effect after printing has obvious unevenness, good coverage and washing resistance. Basically there is not peculiar smell, and the ink is generally used for screen printing of ordinary objects. The biggest feature of this printing process is its high printing accuracy.

 


Silk printing

 

The printing process of environmentally friendly shopping bags generally uses screen printing, which is often referred to as "silk printing." However, in the actual production process, customers often say why some bags have good printing effects and some have poor printing effects. Of course, this has a lot of things which connects with the proficiency of the printing workers and printing technology. And it also connects with the choice of printing ink.

 

Usually silk screen is divided into manual screen printing and machine screen printing, which is a more traditional printing method. This is a commonly used printing method and the price is moderate, so many manufacturers choose this method. This printing method is used to make the film through the logo document, and then the screen can be exposed through the film, and the screen can be made into a printing plate before printing. We know that the printing is very important. If the printing is not good, the final effect will be poor, and there will be burrs.

 

Offset printing

 

For various shopping bags such as non woven cloth bags, offset printing is the abbreviation of machine offset printing. After making a soft offset plate, it is posted on the printing reel of the printing machine, so that one or more bags' logo can be produced for each revolution. This printing method is mainly fast and easy, but the effect is worse than that of silk printing. However, because of its lower price, more and more manufacturers choose this printing method.

 


Thermal transfer process

 

It belongs to special printing in printing. This printing method needs to be completed through an intermediate medium, that is to say, the graphic is printed on the heat transfer film or heat transfer paper, and then the picture is transferred to the fabric such as non-woven fabric etc. through the heat of the transfer equipment. The commonly used medium in textile printing is thermal transfer film. The point is that this printing is exquisite and rich in gradation version, but the printing cost is higher. 

 

Printing technique is one of the important factors for you to create a good custom shopping bag. After all, customized shopping bags with information of company are the best advertising and promotional gifts.
