What Materials are Used to Make Drawstring Backpacks？

Views: 377     Author: Site Editor     Publish Time: 2021-03-09      Origin: Site

Hot selling polyester drawstring bag can be made of any number of materials, which is depending on what you want to use them for and how long.

 

1. Coarse linen

 

Burlap is very strong and easy to care, so many canvas top quality polyester drawstring bags are made of them, which is easy to customize and environmentally friendly. Also, it has been used to make crafts for centuries.

 

Advantages: environmental protection, biodegradable, durable.

Disadvantages: dark material, water resistance.

 

 

2. Cotton

 

Cotton is another popular material to make light weight polyester drawstring bags, which is easy to take care of. Besides, stretcher material helps the 190 polyester fabric drawstring bag withstand daily wear and tear.

 

Advantages: it can be seen anywhere because you can recycle old shirts or any other elastic clothes.

Disadvantages: cotton is usually a thinner material, so it may be more difficult to sew.

 

 

3. Canvas

 

Canvas is an increasingly popular material for the best-selling polyester drawstring bag because it is a cheap option and it is easy to customize with paint, which is also known for its flexibility.

 

Advantages: cheap, easy to use paint or marker, customized.

Disadvantages: hard material, difficult to sew by hand.

 

 

4. Mesh

 

Mesh is a kind of recycle polyester folding drawstring bag that more suitable for indoor use, which is a kind of breathable material. It not only has many colors but also is very light and cheap.

 

Advantages: the mesh is very light and woven is more tightly.

Disadvantages: the presence of holes may not keep your stuff dry.

 

 

5. Nylon

 

Another very popular pull rope backpack material is nylon, which is a smooth material and has many colors. Nylon is relatively resistant to dyeing and easy to care for.

 

Advantages: stain resistance, tight knitting, water resistance.

Disadvantages: it can melt at high temperature of dryer and is not biodegradable.

 

6. Polyester fiber

 

Polyester is a durable fabric that is resistant to fading. Also, it is a lightweight fabric that prevents wrinkles and stains, so you just need to wash and dry the fabric at room temperature.

 

Advantages: color can be maintained for a long time, light enough to float.

Disadvantages: airtight fabric, easy to retain odor.

 

 

7. Flannelette

 

Flannelette personalized sports polyester drawstring bag has the characteristics of easy washing, good air permeability, long use time, reusable, and good advertising effect, but the cost of flannelette bundle pocket is relatively higher than that of other materials. The price of flannelette durable polyester drawstring bag is affected by fabric thickness, printing requirements, and specifications. It can be used for bracelet, necklace, ring, jewelry, jade, cosmetics, perfume, fragrance, dried flowers, candy, electronics, sound, mobile power, and other gifts.

 

 

8. Linen

 

Linen promotional polyester drawstring bag has the characteristics of longer storage time and more reusable times, but linen is more expensive than the above materials. Besides, linen is hard and the washing process is troublesome. Similarly, the price of linen polyester drawstring backpack bag is also affected by size, cloth thickness, and printing.
