Do you know the production process of reusable shopping bags that help protect the environment?

Views: 264     Author: Site Editor     Publish Time: 2021-11-08      Origin: Site

The so-called reusable shopping bags generally refer to shopping bags that can be reused multiple times and whose materials can be naturally degraded. These products are safe and environmentally friendly. In order to build a resource-saving and environment-friendly society, we will take effective measures from the source to urge enterprises to produce durable and easy-to-recycle plastic shopping bags. We try to guide and encourage people to use plastic shopping bags rationally,protect the ecological environment, and further promote energy conservation and emission reduction work.

 

Eco friendly bags generally use screen printing, which is often referred to as "silk printing", which has always been a common printing process for many manufacturers. It is usually printed manually, so the color control on the craft is not easy to control. With the continuous improvement of customers' requirements for exquisite printing of products and environmental protection, many new non-woven printing methods have emerged. Here are some of the mainstream ones on the market.

 

 


Heat transfer

Thermal transfer is a special type of printing in printing. This method requires an intermediate medium, that is, first print the graphics on the thermal transfer film or thermal transfer paper, and then transfer the pattern to the non-woven fabric by heating the transfer device. The commonly used medium in textile printing is thermal transfer film. Its advantages are that it is beautifully printed, has a rich layered version, and is comparable to photos. It is suitable for small area color image printing. The disadvantage is the high price and high printing cost.

 

 

Watermark

It is named after its use of water-based elastic glue as the printing medium. It is more common in textile printing and is also called printing. Blend the color paste and water-based elastic glue when printing. No chemical solvents are used when washing the printing plate, and it can be washed directly with water. It is characterized by good tinting strength, strong covering and fastness, washing resistance, and basically no peculiar smell.

 

 


Gravure printing

The finished products processed in this way are usually called laminated non-woven bags. This process is divided into two steps, that is, the traditional gravure printing process is used to print the image and text on the film, and then the film with the pattern is laminated on the non-woven fabric by the laminating process. Generally, non-woven bags printed with large-area color patterns use this process. It is characterized by exquisite printing, machine production in the whole process, and short production cycle. In addition, the product has excellent waterproof performance and can generally be used as a waterproof bag. The durability of the finished product is also better than that of non-woven bags produced by other processes. The disadvantage is that it conflicts with the concept of environmental protection, because the film is difficult to degrade.

 

Shopping bags in pouch provided in supermarkets are said to be eco-friendly bags. It is very simple for citizens to know the authenticity and falsehood. They only need to put the empty bags in the water. The ones that sink are eco-friendly bags, and the ones that float on the water are non-environmental-friendly bags. Just because of the difference in materials and processes, it is a very simple method.

 

Now through the country's vigorous publicity and the implementation of eco friendly reusable bags in various provinces and cities, environmentally friendly shopping bags have gradually entered the public's field of vision. At the same time, environmental protection also uses shopping bags as an opportunity to cleverly integrate with fashion. ICOM's plain environmental friendly bags are very good to highlight the user's personality. From materials to patterns, all kinds of emerging designs are very popular. A variety of styles (such as eco friendly foldable shopping bags and eco friendly cotton bags)are widely sought after by consumers.
