How To Make Canvas Tote Bag

Views: 238     Author: Site Editor     Publish Time: 2021-06-14      Origin: Site

Canvas tote bag is light and practical, fashionable and practical. People hope to bring us a different experience through the design of this kind of environmental protection bag, pursuing high-quality culture, advocating healthy attitude towards life while entertainment, advocating fashion trend and paying more attention to spiritual and civilized visual enjoyment at the same time. Here is how to make a complete environmental protection canvas bag.

 

Design And Printing Of Canvas Bag

Canvas bag as a new propaganda tool, it is necessary to print beautiful and eye-catching advertising words on the exquisite canvas bag. Designers design patterns according to the requirements of customers.

 

There are two printing methods, one is watermark printing, referred to as screen printing. It is characterized by good coloring power, strong covering, fastness, and no peculiar smell. Another is heat transfer printing, short for hot stamping. It is characterized by exquisite printing, rich sense of hierarchy, comparable to photos, suitable for small area color image printing.

 

Select Fabric

Generally, according to the budget in your mind, you can determine the quantity and color of the cloth, and the range of cloth selection is much narrower. For the same ounce canvas, there are twill, plain and other lines, but the stiffness and vertical and horizontal tension of the cloth are almost the same.

 

Fold The Canvas

When the fabric is laid out, it's time to fold it in half. Place it so that you see 16 inches by 15 inches. Place the crease at the bottom.

 

Cutting Of Canvas Bag

After selecting and preparing the materials, the next task is to cut the large rolls of cloth into small pieces. In the end, it is made into a tote bag. Our cutting master will accurately cut cut the manuscript according to the size of the manuscript, and the cutting error is generally within 1cm.

 

Sewing Of Canvas Bag

When all processes are ready, we need to sew the cut positive piece and side piece one by one into canvas environmental protection bags, and have very strict requirements for sewing, exquisite sewing and quality assurance, so we can meet the needs of large quantities of customized canvas bags.

 

Packaging Of Canvas Bags

After the canvas bags are sewed, the quality inspector will pick out the unqualified ones and the workers will rework or void them. The packer will pack the qualified canvas bags according to the customer's requirements.

 

Personalized Canvas Bag

You will like to browse our large number of customized canvas tote bags, including bags with high quality rope handle, bags with pockets, bags with gussets, jute bags and so on!

 

We can even help you add logos to your wholesale tote bags. We offer you screen printing options as well as digital printing to achieve unlimited color and design.

 

It's your choice. Start equipping your team now! We can't wait to help you start the design process. To learn and apply all DIY crafts you can make with canvas tote bags, we have created the ultimate guide for you, please check it out!
