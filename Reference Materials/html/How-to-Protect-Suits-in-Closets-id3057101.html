How to Protect Suits in Closets?

Views: 265     Author: Site Editor     Publish Time: 2021-07-08      Origin: Site

 

We must take actions to ensure our suits are not exposed to harmful conditions while stored in our closets. In this article, we will introduce several storage suggestions which are common and easy to take into practice.

 

 


Remove Suits from Dry-cleaning Bags

We should always remove suits from dry-cleaning bags. There are many kinds of suit cover bags such as breathable garment bag and spinner garment bag. They are perfect for storing your suits safely in your closet. It will also protect your suits from dust, moths, damp conditions, pleats, pet hair, and any mold or mildew growth. For example, plastic dry-cleaning bags are made from petroleum, which can emit harmful gasses when stored in a closet. Do not have your suits exposed to these gases and trapped inside the plastic when stored for an extended period because it can damage your garments and tarnish their color.

 

 

Brush your Suits Gently before storing

When you are done wearing your suit, you had better brush it off before storing it back in your closet. To keep your suits fresh, let your suit air out for a few hours, or even a day before putting it back into your closet. Otherwise, any dust or odors that might be on your suit from wearing it can get trapped inside. And later when you go to wear it again, your suits not smell so nice.

 

 

Use the Proper Hangers

Avoid using wire hangers as they can cause unnecessary wrinkles on your clothes, especially on your shoulders. I believe nobody want wear a dress shirt and suit coat with strange wrinkles. Instead, you should buy either felt or wooden hangers that are the right fit for your dress clothes. And these hangers can also withhold the weight of your suits to keep them in good condition.

 

 


Leave the Buttons Unbuttoned

When you hang up your suit coat, you should always keep the buttons unbuttoned, not put them on the hanger. Suit protector bag is a good option if you have trouble in putting your suits. This allows for your jacket to breathe and falls freely and naturally as it would on your body. You should make your suit to retain its natural shape as much as possible. Button it when it's hanging, which depends on the length of time it's been hanging in the wardrobe. When you put it on again, it may produce a more rigid and uncomfortable appearance and feeling.

 

 

Clean and Dry Clothes Before Hanging

If your clothing has been exposed to any extremely hot or humid conditions, it will be more likely to cause mold or mildew if stored in your closet while it’s still damp or retaining any moisture. Moreover, the garment bags are also important. Best garment bag for dresses, cotton garment bags, best garment bag for suit storage, etc. are suitable storage of clothes. In addition, any clothing that may have perspiration or stains should not be placed in your closet with your clean clothes. Those perspiration or stains may increase the risk of attracting unwanted insects or stinking up the rest of the clothes in your closet.

 

 

Protect your Suits from Light

We should ensure that our closet can protect our clothes from light as much as possible. For instance, we can position our suits away from bright and natural light. Modoker garment bag, wardrobe bag, etc. will help with this if they are not clear to avoid color damage, heat damage, or faded fabrics.

 

 

These methods mentioned above can help your suits keep fresh and protect them from pesky moths. Meanwhile, choosing the right garment bags is also of great important.
